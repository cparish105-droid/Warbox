Click a nation or a person to inspect them. Use the nation panel to boost tech or intelligence. Workers travel to individual trees, stone outcrops, ore deposits, and crop patches. Every resource has finite reserves and visibly shrinks as it is harvested. Supply trucks move resources between capital and outpost stockpiles. You can plant several rally flags: click a flag to select it (soldiers gather at the selected flag) and delete it with Delete or the nation panel; if a flag is across the water, ships ferry soldiers there. Bombers keep attacking, switching to a fresh target whenever one is destroyed. Harbors launch ships that carry real people: troop ships load soldiers at the pier, sail around islands and land them on an enemy beach, while merchant ships carry traders and cargo and must tie up at a partner's harbor to trade; warships also battle at sea; modern aircraft conduct animated bombing runs against enemy settlements. Industrial nations launch trucks, cars, and tanks; aviation research and airports unlock visible planes; navigation unlocks merchant ships and warships that sail on water routes. Districts grow outward from city centers. Each culture has unique architecture and military traits. Trade pacts exchange real resources; wars and broken alliances are remembered. Technologies modify production, defenses, and combat. Evolution is deliberately slow unless you enable the boost. Nations may start wars automatically when they judge a rival weaker. The flag tool sets a rally point. Wheel zooms; right-drag or hand tool pans.
